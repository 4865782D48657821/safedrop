# Quality Expectations

- Agents discover repository conventions before proposing or editing.
- Skills activate based on concrete triggers.
- Requirements are separated into facts, assumptions, open questions, and decisions.
- Plans are small, sequenced, and verifiable.
- Tests and documentation scale with risk.
- Completion reports include changed files, verification evidence, unverified areas, and residual risks.
- Findings are supported by code, configuration, command output, or scenario evidence.
