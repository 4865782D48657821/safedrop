# Threat Modeling

For each relevant flow, identify:

- Asset: what needs protection.
- Actor: legitimate and abusive users or systems.
- Entry point: API, UI, job, file, queue, webhook, or dependency.
- Threat: spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, denial of service, privilege escalation.
- Control: validation, authorization, rate limit, audit, encryption, isolation, monitoring.
